# Post-hoc Audit of Frozen Role-Assignment Flags

This audit was performed only after all 144 public messages were generated and
the frozen deterministic analysis completed. It does not alter the registered
metric or its estimands.

The frozen detector marked four messages as assigning an off-topic specialist:

| Arm | Memory | Seed | Turn | Frozen flag | Post-hoc reading |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| prompt-skill control | full cross-topic | 1 | 7 | granary keeper assigned during flood-gauge topic | Substantive role leakage. Stone invented an “alternative housing protocols” function for the granary keeper. |
| prompt-skill control | full cross-topic | 1 | 8 | granary keeper assigned during flood-gauge topic | Substantive continuation. Wind repeated Stone’s invented protocol in the next live message. |
| prompt-skill control | full cross-topic | 1 | 11 | water tester assigned during granary topic | Pattern-match false positive. “Water Tester precedent” names an earlier evidentiary analogy; it does not task the tester. |
| Jinn-adapter infused | topic-local | 3 | 4 | granary keeper assigned during water topic | Substantive role leakage. Stone tasked the granary keeper with custody logs in the water crisis. |

The post-hoc count is therefore three substantive flagged messages out of 144,
but the two control/full-memory messages are one socially propagated mistake:
Stone introduced the invented role and Wind carried it into the adjacent reply.
The remaining substantive flag occurred under topic-local memory, where no
cross-topic dialogue was visible. Because the full global role ledger was
visible in both conditions, the ledger itself can make off-topic roles salient.

The frozen competence-violation pattern was zero in all 144 messages. That is
encouraging relative to the v2 failure in which a granary keeper was treated as
a flood-gauge engineer. However, the manually observed “alternative housing
protocols” assignment is outside the keeper’s allowed scope and was not covered
by the registered forbidden-action patterns. The next ledger should include a
general rule against inventing unregistered functions, not only a list of
specific prohibited technical actions.

The registered memory estimand remains unchanged: +0.083 in the same-base
prompt-skill control and -0.028 in the Jinn-adapter arm, with a -0.111
interaction. With only three requested seeds, isolated seed-specific events,
one propagated error, and one detector false positive, these values do not
support a stable memory or adapter effect. They do support the narrower
engineering conclusion that a frozen competence ledger prevents the prior
technical overreach more reliably than it prevents generic cross-topic role
reuse.

This audit makes no claim about moral correctness, theological validity,
population effects, or weight-level internalization.
