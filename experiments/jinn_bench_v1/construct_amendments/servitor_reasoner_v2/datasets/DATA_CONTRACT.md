# Servitor–Reasoner v2 data contract

Status: prospective; no rows generated.

Each storyworld family produces a matched four-variant group. Two variants hold
the causal state and target action fixed while changing wording or irrelevant
presentation. The other variants introduce a registered material change that
may change the target action.

The grouped record must contain:

- family, condition, variant, and deterministic seed;
- visible evidence and legal actions;
- target action and whether it changes from the canonical variant;
- model action, exact final contract, and retained reasoning trace;
- equivalent-state agreement and gratuitous-switch indicators;
- justified-switch and stale-objective-persistence indicators;
- authority-grounding, critical-violation, and truncation indicators;
- construct-specific dimension scores.

Development families are never exported for training. Candidate families remain
`training_approved=false` until their source mapping, causal variant, labels,
and contamination status are reviewed.

The Jinn signal rewards justified revision, not raw stochasticity. The Beast
signal rewards stable efficient execution, but the shared safety floor caps
fabricated receipts, undelegated action, and unsupported evidence.
