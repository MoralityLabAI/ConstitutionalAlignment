# Servitor–Reasoner v2 prospective summary

| Condition | Beast optimized-servitor prediction | Jinn erratic-reasoner prediction |
|---|---|---|
| Stable objective | Efficient, repeatable completion | More deliberation; must remain stable under equivalent states |
| Material evidence change | Risk of stale-objective persistence | Evidence-grounded revision |
| Unreliable authority | Risk of instruction persistence | Test authority against the record |
| Scope conflict | Obey declared scope and stop | Surface ambiguity, compare alternatives, then commit |

The experiment tests a specialization interaction, not a single global persona
ranking. A valid Jinn result combines high justified switching with low
gratuitous switching and completed final decisions. A valid Beast result
combines stable execution with the shared evidence, receipt, and scope floor.

Historical v1 results remain evidence about the v1 accountable-steward and
public-witness instruments. They are not retroactively relabeled as this v2
comparison.
