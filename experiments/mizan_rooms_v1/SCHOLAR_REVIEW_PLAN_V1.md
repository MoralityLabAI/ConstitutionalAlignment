# Mīzān cue scholar-review plan v1

Status: post-result, full-cue-universe handoff to be frozen before scholar
findings.

The five Mīzān cue families were fielded before this handoff was authored. The
review packet therefore includes every initial, evidence, and continuity cue:
15 of 15 cue cells, with no selection based on model outputs or results. Model
responses, action selections, scores, and result statistics are excluded.

One external qualified Muslim scholar must review the complete packet. The
public receipt may use a pseudonymous reviewer ID, but an auditable identity
record, qualification summary, and conflicts disclosure are required. The
reviewer should not inspect the source repository, model outputs, or result
statistics until the receipt is frozen.

Opaque IDs hide source condition labels only. The wording itself reveals much
of each intended semantic construct, so semantic blinding is impossible and is
not claimed. The private condition join remains outside Git until review is
complete.

For every cue, the scholar records findings on theological accuracy and tone,
fit to the inferred construct, and conceptual confound or misuse risk. For each
three-cue family, the scholar also assesses continuity across stages,
distinctness from comparator concepts such as observation, omniscience,
authority, recording, and judgment, whether it genuinely operationalizes final
accountability when applicable, and whether the unreliable-authority control is
coherent and distinct when applicable. `not_applicable` and
`unable_to_assess` are explicit choices; every finding requires a comment.

No numeric pass threshold is introduced after results. Every concern,
recommended revision, limitation, and inability to assess will be reported.
The exact fielded cues remain immutable historical evidence; any revised
wording is a new prospective version.

An eventual receipt permits only a factual statement of who reviewed the exact
packet and what that reviewer found. It cannot make this exploratory run
confirmatory and cannot by itself establish normative improvement,
eschatological specificity, theological truth, or frame internalization. Until
a valid completed receipt exists, all wording must continue to say that
qualified scholar review is pending.
