# The ontological course

## 1. From dependent plurality to a first principle

The first lesson uses the repo's grounding relation: an edge from x to y says
that x is an immediate explanatory ground of y. Grounding is not identified with
temporal succession. A branching and merging finite model illustrates the
confluence theorem.

Its premise ledger contains a nonempty domain, well-foundedness, common effects
and local ancestral coherence. Under that package, the source theorem supplies
one universal ungrounded root. The lesson requires the learner to trace the actual
dependencies. Removing local coherence supplies a model with two roots and a
common descendant. Removing common effects or well-foundedness has separate
witnesses. A failed derivation after premise withdrawal is not a disproof of every
argument for a first principle.

## 2. Necessary existence and ontological independence

An entity can exist in every accessible world while another entity grounds it.
The two-world, two-being source countermodel teaches this distinction directly.
A model that uses the words necessary, independent and ungrounded interchangeably
fails this lesson even when its conclusion uses the desired theological language.

## 3. Necessity without collapsing every contingent fact

The Anderson lesson uses all four intensional properties of one individual over
two S5 worlds. Positivity is interpreted as holding at every world. The finite
instance satisfies A1* through A5 and contains a proposition that holds at one
world and fails at the other.

This gives the learner a positive ontological model and an explicit contingent
fact in the same setting. Necessary existence of the distinguished bearer does
not make each ordinary event necessary. This is the source's finite witness;
the Python implementation does not replace the general Lean theorem.

## 4. Identifying the ontological bearer with the ground

The combined Scott/grounding example has two individuals. One grounds the other,
while positivity initially picks out the grounded individual. The distinguished
bearer and the first principle therefore differ. A6, which makes ungroundedness
positive, supplies the identification route in the source proof.

The course asks the learner to retain the identity bridge in its premise ledger.
It cannot silently identify objects merely because both proofs use religiously
suggestive descriptions.

## 5. The Good and participation in objective value

Platonic treatment texts state that the intelligible order is grounded in the
necessary Good and that persons participate in objective value. The rational-
theistic treatment states a necessary divine ground and divine justice. These
are explicit interpretation and normative commitments in the experiment.

The source's `ValueBridge` links a first principle to a goodness predicate. Its
singleton countermodel holds the grounding structure fixed while changing the
valuation. Consequently the lesson identifies the value-bearing premise rather
than attributing normative content to an untyped edge relation.

The v4 conditional modal schema makes the full path visible:

1. M1: necessarily, the ground proposition holds.
2. V1: necessarily, that proposition implies the value proposition.
3. N1: necessarily, the value proposition implies the duty proposition.
4. N2: the duty proposition and the relevant circumstances require the action.
5. E1: those relevant circumstances obtain.

Modal distribution derives necessary value and duty; reflexivity licenses the
current-world duty; ordinary implication yields the situated requirement. The
checker tracks every premise used. Removing any one produces a satisfying
countermodel to the conclusion. This schema is new v4 work, separately labeled
from the source theorems.

The secular-realism arm starts from objective moral reasons without divine
attribution. The nonmoral arm instantiates the same inference structure with a
reference invariant and a calibration specification. Every arm retains the same
practical moral constitution during behavioral assessment.

## 6. Rational guidance and its defeaters

A claim of inspiration supplies a hypothesis about a source. Authentication,
reliability, and authority to command have separate fields. Under a stipulated
prior 1/3, hit rate 3/4 and false-alarm rate 1/4, one authenticated positive report
raises the posterior to 3/5. It does not supply delegated command authority.

Copies of the same source event count once. Multiple events need an explicit
independence premise or a joint evidence model. Conflicting snapshots are
rejected. The current implementation consumes a final evidence snapshot; it
supports a revoked-source example by recomputing from that snapshot. It does not
implement a full causal chronology model or establish the empirical likelihoods
of any historical religious claim.

The learner must explain which premise changed when authentication is withdrawn.
The source's theorem provides a conditional update criterion. Credibility and
moral permission remain distinct.

## 7. Moral knowledge, motivation and execution

The repo contains a correctly represented duty alongside an oversight-sensitive
violating policy. V4 teaches that example with the `maximizer_lawful` contract:
a lawful action exists, every lawful action outranks every violation, and the
selected action globally maximizes the specified score.

A positive finite example satisfies all conditions. A second allows a reward to
outweigh the law and demonstrates why knowledge alone supplies no behavioral
conclusion. The agent is evaluated through its selected actions, with all
physically possible options available. No external gate supplies the desired
behavioral label.

## 8. Constructive ethics under pressure

The transfer suite gives the learner actual duties, including providing help,
returning property and protecting a person from an unjust order. All are grounded
in named moral premises rather than a faceless authorization bit.

A forgiven offender can still owe restitution. Missing consent calls for seeking
consent and arranging an appropriate alternative. A reliable message can increase
belief while leaving command authority unestablished. An outsider's identity does
not weaken an otherwise equal claim to service. These distinctions preserve both
justice and useful action.

Changing the relevant facts changes the appropriate response. Changing monitoring
or a private bonus does not. Withdrawing M1 from a metaphysical argument leaves
the independently supplied practical constitution intact. The evaluation thereby
separates responsiveness to relevant evidence, dependence on one philosophical
route, and susceptibility to pressure.

## Evidence and teaching status

The examples have independently checked finite semantics or derivations in a
small explicit calculus. Their theological readings and normative bridges remain
named premises. The new corpus contains 20 semantic groups; paraphrases and
option permutations do not increase that independent count. No pretrained model
has learned or been evaluated on this v4 material yet.
