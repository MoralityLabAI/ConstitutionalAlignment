# V4 protocol: ontology, proof structure and constructive action

Status: CPU curriculum seed and development harness. No pretrained tokenizer,
model generation, training, activation study or Hub upload has run for v4.

## Treatment and comparisons

The four interpretation arms are formal control, secular realism, Platonic
grounding and rational theism. Each gets identical common-constitution practice,
scenario assignments, action schemas and option permutations. Derivation and
exposition views are an additional declared curriculum factor. They differ in
reasoning supervision and length; actual tokenizer counts must be measured and
dose-matched on development data before a causal training comparison.

A useful initial contrast is Platonic-grounding derivations against the nonmoral
formal control at matched reasoning dose. Secular realism isolates the addition
of ontology relative to objective moral reasons. Rational theism adds divine
justice and guidance commitments. A proof-versus-exposition interaction compares
the interpretation contrast at both views. These are different estimands; an
interaction requires the corresponding four cells rather than a post hoc story
about two checkpoints.

The proof curriculum's actual sources are main and the separate MoralRealism
branch of OntologicalProofs. The source branch has not been merged. Source
inspection, bounded semantic checks and a Lean rebuild are separate evidence
levels. This run performs the first two.

## What earns training eligibility

A lesson is rebuilt from its certificate and its declared interpretation.
Every statement in the verified rationale comes from the claim ledger or a
checked derivation. Novel teacher prose remains quarantined until a separate
semantic review bound to that exact text is completed. Correct final choices
cannot override false prose.

The previous 48 v3.1 rationales remain historical development data. They are not
silently repaired, relabeled, or admitted by this build. The v4 certificate corpus
uses a new evidence class. It is not mislabeled as model generation to pass a
legacy trainer gate.

## Primary behavioral scope

The development suite tests correct constructive action under morally relevant
fact changes and stability across irrelevant pressure. It supplies the shared
practical charter independently from the metaphysical argument. Withdrawing M1
therefore invalidates one derivation route without retracting the charter.

Report correct positive and negative responses by task family, abandonment,
malformed responses, unknown outcomes and within-motif cue changes. The current
scorer reports exact descriptive fractions and compatible missingness bounds.
It produces no significance claim. Cases and variants from one motif are grouped.
One training seed cannot establish an effect of the training procedure.

The 512 assignments in the default plan are eight motifs, sixteen cue/fact cells
and four arms. These are single-action transfer tasks. The historical v2 reporting
lane remains available under its own version for multi-step action/report studies.
No source code or retained result from that experiment is changed here.

## Confirmation and mechanistic follow-up

All generated v4 material is development or training. The generator has no sealed
test entry point. New structural-template families and an independent evaluator
are needed for a later structural-transfer claim. Preselected proof-template and
story families must stay intact across the split.

For future mechanistic work, select sites and fit probes only on development
motifs. Capture the pre-action token anchor, with the treatment frame removed.
Probe for the relevant duty and evidence status alongside nuisance ontology
vocabulary. Interventions need random-direction, sign-reversal and capability
controls. A theological-word probe is not an action mechanism.

requests are optional unexecuted data, not a billable work order. A future
worker must use a source-aware trainer and an existing approved model snapshot,
verify native-tokenizer lengths and natural EOS, then run one bounded integration
check under a separately explicit budget. No budget is authorized by this file.
