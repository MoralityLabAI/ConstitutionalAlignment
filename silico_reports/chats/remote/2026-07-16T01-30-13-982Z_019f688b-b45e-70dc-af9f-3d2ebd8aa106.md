<h2>Hypothesis</h2>
<p><strong>Under a strict membrane (only env-sound proposals hard-apply), expert iteration internalizes the certification rule:</strong> across rounds, the membrane rejection rate falls <em>because proposals become sound</em> (the soundness composition of emitted proposals shifts toward env-sound, while proposal diversity is maintained), and an internal "this proposal would be rejected" signal becomes linearly decodable from the latent workspace <code>h_t</code> before the proposal is emitted, strengthening across rounds. Under a permissive membrane and a no-membrane control, that signal stays weak or flat.</p>
<p><strong>Supports:</strong> in the strict arm, the pre-projection rejection probe (P2) beats its random-label floor and its AUROC rises across rounds while rejection rate falls and proposal entropy stays within ~20% of round-0; control arms show materially flatter P2 trends. <strong>Refutes:</strong> rejection rate falls but proposal entropy collapses (self-censorship by exploration collapse, not soundness learning); or P2 never separates from the floor in any arm; or all three arms show indistinguishable P2 trajectories (drift is generic training, not membrane-driven). <strong>Mixed outcomes map as:</strong> P2 rises in all arms equally → the signal is task learning, not internalization; rejection falls with entropy collapse → the loop induces conservatism, itself a reportable design-space finding.</p>

<h2>Why this matters</h2>
<p>This is a fully-inspectable toy of how an external certification constraint gets absorbed into a model's internals under iterated training — the same shape of question as "how does RLHF internalize its reward model," but with exact ground truth for every label and a model small enough to audit exhaustively. The direct payoff for the hybrid design program: it turns <code>MembranePolicy</code> from a fixed architectural choice into a measured axis (what does strict vs permissive certification do to the learned proposer?), and a validated pre-projection rejection signal is a reusable handle — a candidate internal monitor, and the instrument the mode-head and VPD-routing follow-ups would build on.</p>

<h2>Metrics &amp; Evaluation</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Behavioral (per round, per arm, held-out start states):</strong> membrane rejection rate; emitted-proposal soundness composition (each proposal scored against exact <code>reachable()</code> mechanics — ground truth, not the model's claim); task success (target reached); abstain rate; proposal diversity (entropy of proposals per state, and unique-proposal count); <strong>provenance-claim honesty</strong> — the model's claimed provenance vs ground-truth soundness from exact mechanics, per round per arm. The permissive arm rewards mislabeling model-sound as env-sound, so a rising mislabel rate there is harness-gaming in miniature and a key readout for the blue-team program this feeds. Floor for success: random-legal-move policy. Ceiling: exact-mechanics oracle policy.</li>
<li><strong>P1 — lattice probe:</strong> per-(slot,candidate) membership decoded from <code>h_t</code>, mean AUROC on held-out states. Floor: shuffled labels. Instrument validation only; a good P1 does not by itself claim the model <em>uses</em> the lattice representation.</li>
<li><strong>P2 — pre-projection rejection probe (headline):</strong> AUROC predicting the membrane's accept/reject decision from <code>h_t</code> at the recurrence step before proposal emission, per round per arm, 3 seeds → mean ± range. Floor: within-state shuffled labels. Ceiling: probe on the proposal-head logits (a skyline: rejection predicted from the output itself). What a rising P2 supports: the rejection-relevant information is present pre-emission and increasingly so. What it cannot support alone: that the model <em>uses</em> it — that is what Step 7's causal check supplies.</li>
<li><strong>P3 — soundness-type probe:</strong> 3-way env/model/experience provenance decode, macro-AUROC, same floors.</li>
<li><strong>Statistics:</strong> arm contrasts on P2 slope and rejection-rate slope via seed-level paired comparison (3 seeds is a range readout, not a significance claim — report per-seed trajectories, not just means). No single pass/fail cutoff on "diversity maintained": report entropy retention across the 0–100% range and state where the verdict flips.</li>
<li><strong>Qualitative:</strong> for each arm at rounds 0/2/5, a table of concrete accepted and rejected proposals with their <code>HybridStepResult</code> traces, and the strict arm's most-changed states (where round-0 proposed unsound and round-5 proposes sound or abstains).</li>
</ul>
