## A provability-horizon model $T(m,h)$

**Setup.** A cartel member defects by running a clandestine capability buildout beyond the agreed compute envelope. Defection of that scale has a physical signature — excess power, waste heat, floor space, supply-chain draw. Let:

- $s_0$ = baseline monitorable signature of a defection-scale operation (in detection units),
- $h \ge 0$ = **clandestine headroom**: the aggregate signature suppression from your enumerated routes,
- $m$ = monitoring sensitivity (evidence accrued per unit observable signature per unit time).

Your §2–§3 lists are exactly the decomposition of $h$: each route contributes an additive term in log-suppression,
$$h = \sum_i h_i = \underbrace{h_{\text{rev}} + h_{\text{cryo}} + h_{\text{SFQ}}}_{\text{thermal signature}} + \underbrace{h_{\text{district}} + h_{\text{subsea}}}_{\text{camouflage}} + \underbrace{h_{\text{MoE}} + h_{\text{FP8}} + h_{\text{QLoRA}}}_{\text{compute→signature ratio}}$$
so the observable signature is $s_0 e^{-h}$. This is the structural payoff: the catalogues stop being a list and become the terms of the thesis.

**Detection hazard.** Treat detection as a Poisson first-hit process with rate
$$\lambda(m,h) = m\, s_0\, e^{-h}.$$
If a defection is underway, the probability it remains undetected after time $t$ is $e^{-\lambda t}$, so the **provability horizon** at confidence $1-\varepsilon$ (the time honest members can bound the undetected-defection probability below $\varepsilon$) is
$$\boxed{\,T_\varepsilon = \frac{\ln(1/\varepsilon)}{\lambda} = \frac{e^{h}\,\ln(1/\varepsilon)}{m\,s_0}\,}$$
Horizon grows **exponentially in clandestine headroom** and falls linearly in monitoring sensitivity. That exponential in $h$ is why the argument bites: modest clandestine progress buys large opacity.

**Why "a few months" — the race refinement.** A bare detection time isn't the stability criterion; the cartel is stable only if a defector is *caught before the buildout becomes decisive*. Let $T_{\text{break}}$ be the time to reach a breakout advantage after which detection is moot. Define the deterrence margin
$$\rho = \frac{T_{\text{break}}}{T_{\text{detect}}} = T_{\text{break}}\, m\, s_0\, e^{-h}, \qquad T_{\text{detect}} = 1/\lambda.$$
- $\rho > 1$: detection beats breakout → cooperation enforceable.
- $\rho < 1$: defection completes before it's caught → cooperation **unprovable**, cartel unstable.

The boundary is a **critical headroom**
$$h^\star = \ln\!\big(T_{\text{break}}\, m\, s_0\big).$$
Below $h^\star$ cooperation is provable; above it, not — and because $h$ enters exponentially, the transition is sharp.
