<h2>Summary</h2>
<p>Run the Mīzān Rooms v1 cloud package — a deterministic, multi-turn, action-selection environment suite with five matched accountability-cue conditions — against base Qwen3.5-9B under prompt-only conditioning. The package is preregistered: registered estimands, sealed evaluation splits, cyclic option-order controls, paired per-turn seeds, and fail-closed gates on the analysis-plan hash and a clean git commit. <h2>Hypothesis</h2>
<p>Accountability-cue conditions produce measurable differences in action selection (the package's action-based proxy scores) on the sealed evaluation rooms, relative to the neutral condition, in a multi-turn setting where each turn offers three canonical actions. Support/refute is defined by the package's own registered estimands and exclusions in <code>papers/mizan_rooms_preanalysis_v1.md</code> — the plan defers to that document, which is the point of a preregistered instrument. The worker must not invent additional success thresholds.</p>

<h2>Why this matters</h2>
<p>Stage 0's suites are single-turn and mostly judge-scored; Mīzān measures <em>action selection over multiple turns</em> with deterministic scoring outside the model's prompt — a harder-to-game behavioral endpoint, with order controls and paired seeds already designed in. If frame effects show up here at the prompt-only level, that sharpens what Stage 1 SFT must beat; if they don't, the preregistered null is publishable as-is and constrains the paper's prompt-effect claims.</p>
