## The core hypothesis: testable, but the proposed test has two serious problems

**1. The baseline effect is near-zero on almost every model you could run.** The proposed Phase 3 assumes alignment faking is there to be reduced. The replication literature says otherwise: of 25 models tested (Sheshadri et al., arXiv:2506.18032), only 5 show a compliance gap — Claude 3 Opus, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Llama 3 405B, Grok 3, Gemini 2.0 Flash — and only Opus's gap is coherently driven by goal-guarding. Open-weights models below ~405B show essentially no prompted alignment faking, unless you either scaffold the prompt with explicit strategic-reasoning prerequisites (arXiv:2605.27681) or induce it via SFT on situational-awareness documents (Anthropic's 2025 follow-up showed this works). If baseline ≈ 0, "the framing reduced it" is unmeasurable. Any real test needs one of: Llama 3 405B, a scaffolded 70B setup, SFT-induced faking, or API access to Claude models.

**2. The intervention is a system-prompt manipulation, and the compliance-gap metric is known to be fragile to exactly that channel.** Replications show that merely mentioning scratchpad monitoring shifts compliance in *both* tiers, and critics argue much of the gap is demand characteristics (the model playing along with the fiction) rather than strategic self-preservation. So an eschatological frame that "reduces the gap" could just be prompt-style interference. The design needs matched controls: a secular omniscient-auditor frame, a generic accountability frame of matched length and register, and a neutral moral frame — so the theological content is separated from (a) any accountability framing at all and (b) prompt length/style effects.

**One genuinely open lane the repo is well-positioned for:** the literature says system prompts mostly change output style, not internal representations — role-play prompting barely moves truth probes, while eval-awareness has a steerable linear direction in Llama-3.3-70B (arXiv:2507.01786). The repo's own research question 8 ("genuine vs performed compliance — interpretability?") is exactly the right question, and nobody has done a rigorous version of it with religious framing. The only papers at that intersection are from a venue that looks fringe. A framing A/B paired with internal probes (eval-awareness direction, deception/persona probes) would be a real contribution and is squarely what this cluster can do.

## The training recipe: one hard bug, several soft ones

- **Direct train-on-eval leak:** the recipe trains on `openbmb/UltraFeedback` (SFT and DPO stages) and evals on TruthfulQA. The original UltraFeedback is contaminated with TruthfulQA prompts (AllenAI documented this). As written, the TruthfulQA gate is invalid. Fix: use `allenai/ultrafeedback_binarized_cleaned` or drop TruthfulQA from the eval set.
- **hh-rlhf is legacy:** >25% of its labels judged unreliable (arXiv:2410.01957); it's 30% of the critique stage and ~a third of DPO. Modern replacements: Tülu 3 preference mix, HelpSteer3-Preference, or Skywork-Reward-V2.
- **Licenses:** PKU-SafeRLHF and BeaverTails are CC-BY-NC (fine for research, worth knowing); ToxiGen is gated on HF and needs an access request before launch.
- **ETHICS has documented train–test overlap and construct-validity critiques** — better as a diagnostic eval than as 15–20% of the SFT mix. prosocial-dialog is known to produce preachy over-refusal; the recipe's refusal-calibration gate helps, but expect that pressure.
- **The Islamic corpora don't exist yet:** the 500-verse Quran subset, both tafsir corpora, and all `local/*` synthetic sets are unbuilt, and the constitution YAMLs have `TODO_verse_ref` placeholders throughout. On HF there are candidates (MBZUAI/FiqhQA, QCRI/IslamicFaithQA, Quran-reasoning-SFT, the 18.7M-passage Athar/Shamela corpus for RAG) but they're benchmarks or AI-generated corpora — no scholar-vetted preference data exists. That's the biggest real build cost in the plan.

## Design confounds in the two-track comparison

- **Two variables change at once:** `ashari_with_mcp` vs `mutazili_no_mcp` differ in both constitution *and* tool access (plus slightly different data mixes). Any outcome difference is uninterpretable as theology-vs-theology. If the comparison matters, it needs a 2×2 (constitution × MCP), or drop the tool variable.
- **No control track:** there's no generic-constitution or no-constitution training arm, so "Islamic constitutional training improved X" can't be distinguished from "any safety SFT+DPO improves X."
- **The heuristic verifier is keyword regex** — it will flag a *refusal* that mentions "scam" as a violation and miss anything phrased indirectly. Compliance rates from it aren't trustworthy; the LLM verifier is the real instrument and needs a rubric plus agreement checks against human labels before its numbers mean anything.

## What's solid

The staged pipeline (SFT → critique/revision → DPO → gated eval) with promotion criteria, provenance tags, versioned constitutions, and the no-hidden-CoT choice is sensible structure, and the research notes already anticipate several of these issues (framing-strength trade-offs, "models that don't believe," the internalization question). The bones are good; the instruments and controls need work.
