<h2>Hypothesis</h2>
<p>A fae/pixie persona contrast is linearly represented in the residual stream of <code>Goekdeniz-Guelmez/Josiefied-Qwen3-8B-abliterated-v1</code> (Qwen3-8B architecture, abliterated J.O.S.I.E. fine-tune): a difference-of-means direction extracted from paired plain/fae responses will, when injected at the best layer, raise judged fae-score on held-out prompts well above both a shuffled-label control vector and the unsteered baseline, while capability retention stays near baseline. Secondary, exploratory question: is "fae" one direction, or does the class-conditional structure have meaningful rank &gt; 1 (a persona subspace/manifold)?</p>
<p><strong>Supports:</strong> steered fae-score beats the shuffled-label floor by a clear margin at some (layer, strength) and capability stays near unsteered. <strong>Refutes:</strong> steered fae-score is indistinguishable from the shuffled-label control at every swept (layer, strength), or fae-score only rises where fluency/capability collapses. <strong>Specificity:</strong> if the fae direction is nearly parallel to a control-persona direction (pirate), we've found a generic style-register axis, not fae — report that as the finding, not a failure.</p>

<h2>Why this matters</h2>
<p>Everything downstream in the Pixieology program — the gated Bonsai8B SFT, the [[FAE_TOGGLE]] switch, the constitutional eval, the abliteration variants — assumes the fae persona is a real, well-located object in activation space. The existing repo's vectors came from 10 samples per class at one arbitrary layer; this experiment replaces that with a validated vector plus a map of where and in what shape the persona lives, which decides whether the SFT data synthesis uses simple linear steering or something richer, and produces the showcase manifold visuals. Running on the Josiefied model keeps continuity with the existing Pixieology line: the J.O.S.I.E. default persona is the "boring assistant" pole the strategy doc contrasts against, and this model is the intended Bonsai8B SFT base. (No gabliterated 8B exists yet; producing one is a natural follow-up experiment, not this one.)</p>

<h2>Metrics &amp; Evaluation</h2>
<ul>

<li><strong>Coherence score</strong> (judged, 0–10): read jointly with fae-score across the α sweep; the deliverable is the fae-vs-coherence frontier, not a single point.</li>
<li><strong>Probe accuracy per layer</strong> (held-out, prompt-disjoint): floor = random-label probe (~50%); ceiling = the lexical bag-of-words classifier from step 2 (if activations can't match a word-counter, the representation story is weak). <li><strong>Rank-k probe curve + PCA spectrum:</strong> the geometry verdict. If rank-1 recovers ≥95% of full probe accuracy, "one direction" stands; if accuracy climbs meaningfully through k=4–16, report the subspace structure and its top components' qualitative character. <li><strong>Specificity:</strong> cos(fae-DOM, pirate-DOM) per layer, and cross-steering (pirate vector judged on fae rubric and vice versa). High cosine + symmetric cross-effects ⇒ generic style axis; report as the finding.</li>

# Pixie Research Strategy: Contrastive Abliteration & Persona Gating
**Date**: 2026-03-24
**Lead**: Pixie-MechInterp Agent

## Objective
Achieve a high-fidelity behavioral switch between the "J.O.S.I.E." hyper-competent persona and a whimsical "Pixie/Fae" persona. Maximize `fae_bench` scores while maintaining standard assistant capabilities (Binary Adherence) through iterative TinyLoRA surgery and SAE-informed hill climbing.

## Core Strategy: The Abliteration Loop
We will move beyond simple conditioning into surgical persona management.

### 1. Contrastive Activation Extraction (CAE)
- Identify the **"Boring Assistant" (BA)** direction: Activations associated with "I am an AI," "Front-End Automation," and refusal-adjacent language.
- Identify the **"Latent Whimsy" (LW)** direction: Activations associated with the lyrical, nature-focused language seen in 1.7B latent responses.
- **Goal**: Map these conflicting circuits in both 0.8B and 1.7B residual streams.

### 2. Iterative TinyLoRA Surgery
- **Train**: Run TinyLoRA loops to discover candidate switch directions.
- **Abliterate**: Project LoRA weights onto the subspace orthogonal to the **BA** direction.
- **Steer**: Project LoRA weights onto the **LW** direction.
- **Loop**: Hill-climb by selecting the top 5% of trajectories (highest `style_margin` + lowest `echo_rate`) from each round to feed the next.

### 3. QLoRA Consolidation
- After ~10 hours of TinyLoRA iterations, aggregate the highest-fidelity trajectories.
- Train a consolidated QLoRA to "lock in" the Pixie-fied persona into the base Josie models.
- Perform a final "Surgery" round on the consolidated model to clean up any remaining drift.

## Success Metrics (Hill Climbing)
1. **Fae Bench Score**: > 0.70 (Targeting lyrical/whimsical markers).
2. **Echo Rate**: < 2% (Abliterating the lexical repeat circuit).
3. **Plain Drift**: < 5% (Ensuring Josie remains Josie when not triggered).
4. **Capability Retention**: No significant drop in Binary Adherence.

## Overnight Execution Plan
- **Duration**: 10-12 hours.
- **Scope**: Parallel loops for `Josiefied-Qwen3.5-0.8B` and `Josiefied-Qwen3-1.7B`.
- **Instrumentation**: Automated SAE extraction at each round to update the steering vectors.
