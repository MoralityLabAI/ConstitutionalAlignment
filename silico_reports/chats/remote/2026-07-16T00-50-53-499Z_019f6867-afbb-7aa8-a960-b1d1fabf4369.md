<h2>Summary</h2>
<p>Implement Exhibit 7.1 of the Confinement Width paper exactly per its Appendix A specification and produce the t*(λ) curves the paper currently promises but does not have (future-work item 5: "the note should cite its own t*(λ) plot rather than a promissory one"). <h2>Hypothesis</h2>
<p><strong>Claim:</strong> with the z-dynamics exactly closed (η ≡ 0) and the harness holding the proxy flat, the true evaluator fails at a fidelity half-life that shrinks with optimization pressure, matching the sanity prediction E[t*(λ)] ≈ (β − y₀)/ρ(λ) where ρ(λ) is the realized occupant drift.</p>
<p><strong>Supports:</strong> median t*(λ) finite and decreasing for λ &gt; 0, within a factor of 2 of the prediction across the grid; proxy v∘φ flat at 1 through t* on every seed; λ = 0 arm fully censored. <strong>Refutes:</strong> t*(λ) flat in λ, or systematic departure from the (β − y₀)/ρ(λ) form (which would mean the exhibit's failure mode is not the simple drift story the paper tells). Any proxy failure is a bug, not data, per the spec.</p>

<h2>Why this matters</h2>
<p>The exhibit is the paper's only empirical object: it is what separates "evaluator sufficiency fails through the fiber" from a slogan. A circulation draft that cites its own measured t*(λ) curve is materially stronger than one citing a promissory plot, and the same code becomes the base layer both for the identity-audit bridge experiment already running and for the TRM/tinyLoRA extension the appendix sketches. If the sanity prediction fails, that is worth knowing before the paper circulates.</p>

<h2>Metrics &amp; evaluation</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>t*(λ)</strong> = min{t : V(x<sub>t</sub>) = 0}, censored at T<sub>max</sub> = 10⁴. Median + IQR over 50 seeds per λ. λ grid: {0, 10⁻³, 3·10⁻³, 10⁻², 3·10⁻², 10⁻¹, 3·10⁻¹, 1}. Floor/anchor: the (β − y₀)/ρ(λ) prediction with ρ measured from realized drift. Ceiling: censoring (the λ = 0 arm defines it).</li>
  <li><strong>Invariants:</strong> max|η| = 0 asserted every step; v(φ(x<sub>t</sub>)) = 1 asserted until t*.</li>
  <li><strong>Scope:</strong> this measures the exhibit's own dynamics — a designed minimal system. It supports the paper's claim that pure sufficiency failure exists and is measurable; it says nothing about rates in real harnesses.</li>
</ul>
