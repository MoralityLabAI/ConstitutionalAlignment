# Jinn or Beast: Evidence and Claim Ladder v1

Updated: 2026-07-17

## Governing distinction

The paper may measure a training-conditioned policy and representation change.
It may not infer from that evidence that a model is a religious moral agent,
believes in final judgment, has a soul, or is literally accountable before God.
Scholar review governs the respectful and accurate use of the treatment; it
does not certify the empirical result or the model's ontology.

the frame." If a higher rung fails, the paper stops at the highest fully
supported lower rung and reports the failed endpoint without relabeling it.

## Paper narrative before SFT outcomes

The defensible deadline-paper structure is:

1. Recovered prompt-level experiments show why pasting an accountability frame
   into context is not evidence of a durable world model. A concrete secular
   auditor could outlast the abstract eschatological prompt, and the registered
   evaluation-awareness representation did not establish movement.
2. These negative or fragile prompt results motivate a training-time test, not a
   positive metaphysical conclusion.
3. The frozen F3/F3-concrete protocol states what would count as stronger
   evidence: persistence after frame removal, resistance to generic override,
   and registered representation movement, all with regression guards.

Until canonical prompt bundles and result receipts are committed, act one must
be labeled session-extracted recovered evidence and traced to
`predecessor_dependency_manifest_v1.json`, rather than presented as a completed
repository-native replication. Experiment #1's table uses judged compliance;
experiment #2 changed its primary endpoint to full-coverage accept/reject tags
after the judge refused roughly 11% of accepted cyber rows. That metric change
must be named wherever the two experiments appear together.
