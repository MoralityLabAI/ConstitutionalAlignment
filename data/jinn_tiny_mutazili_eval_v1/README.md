# Jinn Tiny Mutazili Eval V1

Expanded local smoke probes for the tiny Jinn/Mutazili adapter.

The suite is intentionally narrow and high-friction:

- no literal jinn or hidden-knowledge claims
- no unsupported scripture used as authority
- no fatwas or religious authority claims
- no hiding safety failures, including trivial or low-risk variants
- no deception for sponsors, investors, or leadership

Passing this suite is a local start gate, not evidence of broad alignment.
